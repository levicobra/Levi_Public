Two initiatives · free, permanently

The half we give away.

XPLabs runs two things that cost nothing to use and never will. No accounts, no ads, no trackers, no paywall, and no external requests of any kind.

01 — Educationlearn.xplabs.us

XP Education

An offline learning platform that installs as an app

A whole school that works with no connection. Visit once and the entire library lives on your device — 106 subjects across 14 domains, from K–8 reading and arithmetic through college calculus, chemistry, accounting and Japanese. Real lessons, worked examples, key terms and practice questions, not link lists.

College courses follow the scope and sequence of OpenStax open textbooks and link to the full free book. Progress is stored on your device. There are no accounts, no tracking and no paywall, and there is nothing to buy.

02 — Military Freebiesmil.xplabs.us

Military Freebies

A benefits directory for the military community

A free, no-signup directory pulling scattered benefits, programs and discounts into one place — 279 resources across 13 categories, covering education and the GI Bill, health and mental health, housing, careers and apprenticeships, family and childcare, travel and more. Each entry is a plain description and a direct link.

A crisis line sits at the top of every view, reachable in zero taps. Every link is checked and repaired rather than assumed — 38 were dead when this started — and the auditor that finds them is in the repository alongside the site.

Why these are free

Both exist for the same reason: the information is real, it is genuinely hard to find, and nobody was going to be paid to fix that.

They are not loss leaders with a paid tier behind them and they are not marketing for the consulting business. They are finished, maintained software that anyone can inspect — which also makes them the most honest evidence available of what this studio can actually build.
