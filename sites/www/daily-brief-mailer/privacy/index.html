Privacy policy

Morning Receipt — Daily Brief Mailer

App homePrivacy policyTerms of use

This policy covers Morning Receipt — Daily Brief Mailer, the private email-delivery utility operated by XP Labs. It has no public signup.

Google access and purpose

The mailer requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. It uses this permission to send reviewed Daily Brief news PDFs from the owner's connected Gmail account to owner-authorized recipients. It receives Google's send result, such as a message identifier, to record whether Google accepted the message.

It does not request permission to read incoming mail, search the mailbox, or access recipients' Google accounts. The owner handles replies outside the mailer.

Limited Use

Morning Receipt — Daily Brief Mailer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only for the disclosed briefing-delivery function and its operation and troubleshooting. It is not sold or used for advertising. The local sender script makes no AI API calls.

Local information and protection

The owner's computer stores sender and recipient configuration, reviewed PDFs, prepared email messages and delivery plans. Delivery records include dates, recipient addresses, status, file hashes and provider message or thread identifiers. These records support review and duplicate-send prevention.

Enrollment stores the OAuth refresh credential and client configuration locally using Windows Data Protection API encryption for the current Windows user. Access tokens are used in memory to authorize requests. Credentials and tokens are not included in briefing emails or delivery logs.

The prepared messages, PDFs, configuration and delivery records are ordinary local files protected by the computer's access controls; the mailer does not separately encrypt those files. The original downloaded OAuth client configuration is managed separately by the owner.

Sharing and delivery

Google processes the outgoing email, recipient address and PDF attachments to send the briefing. Authorized recipients and their email providers receive the message and attachments. The mailer does not intentionally transfer this information to data brokers or advertising services.

The owner's Codex coordinator, provided by OpenAI, may process limited delivery-status and error records to operate and troubleshoot delivery. These operational records may include the recipient, date, provider message identifier, file hashes, status and error details. They do not include OAuth credentials or inbox contents.

These public information pages do not contain recipient lists, briefing attachments or OAuth credentials.

Control, retention and deletion

The owner controls the recipient list and can stop future sending, revoke the app's connection in Google Account connections, and delete the local enrollment. Local configuration, prepared messages, PDFs and delivery records remain until the owner removes them.

To stop delivery or request deletion of your recipient information, reply to a briefing or use the XP Labs contact information. The owner handles these requests directly. Revoking access or deleting local files does not recall delivered email or remove copies retained by Google, recipients or their email providers.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the mailer's data handling changes. Any new Google access or use requires appropriate disclosure and authorization before it begins.

Effective November 12, 2026
