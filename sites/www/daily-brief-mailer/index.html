Morning Receipt — Daily Brief Mailer

App homePrivacy policyTerms of use

A private, owner-operated XP Labs utility that emails reviewed Daily Brief news PDFs to recipients the owner authorizes.

One briefing, two formats

The mailer sends the current dated Daily Brief as an illustrated Letter-size PDF and an 80 mm receipt PDF through the owner's connected Gmail account. It is configured to attach only these two reviewed news editions.

This is a private email-delivery utility. There is no public signup or automatic recipient enrollment.

Send-only Google access

The owner authorizes the Google gmail.send permission to send the briefing. The mailer does not request access to read the inbox. Recipients do not sign in or grant access to their own Google accounts.

Read the privacy policy for access, storage, sharing and deletion details, and the terms of use for operation and delivery limits.

Questions or stopping delivery

Reply to the sender of a briefing to ask a question or request that delivery stop. The owner handles replies directly; the mailer does not read them. You can also use the XP Labs contact information.
