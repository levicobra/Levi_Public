Morning Receipt — Daily Brief Mailer

App homePrivacy policyTerms of use

Morning Receipt — Daily Brief Mailer is a private utility operated by XP Labs for sending reviewed Daily Brief news PDFs through the owner's connected Gmail account. It is not a public email service and has no public signup.

Owner-controlled delivery

The owner controls the Google connection, approved briefing files and recipient list. Receiving a briefing does not create an account or authorize the mailer to access the recipient's Google account.

The mailer is configured to send only the current Daily Brief Letter and 80 mm PDF editions after file review. The owner may pause or stop sending.

Delivery limits

Delivery depends on the owner's computer, Google authorization and the receiving email service. A particular delivery time or uninterrupted service is not promised. Provider acceptance does not confirm that a message reached the recipient's inbox or was read.

An uncertain send outcome is held for review rather than automatically retried, to reduce duplicate messages.

Information and sources

The briefing summarizes information available at its stated cutoff. News, prices and availability can change; consult the linked original sources for current details. Receiving a briefing grants no additional rights to third-party material.

Questions and stopping delivery

Recipients can reply to the sender to ask questions or request that delivery stop. The owner reviews replies directly. The privacy policy explains data handling and deletion requests. Public contact details are on the XP Labs website.
