Levi Colby

Founder of XPLabs. I build games, and I build free things that should exist.

XPLabs is a small studio with four titles in progress — a mobile survivors game in Unreal Engine, a puzzle-defense campaign and a life simulator on iPhone, and a live Roblox world. The engineering side of that is on the engineering page, and the games themselves are in the catalog.

Two of the projects are not games and are not for sale. XP Education is a free offline learning platform: 106 subjects from K–8 through college, that work with no connection, no account and no tracking. The military benefits directory pulls hundreds of scattered programs for service members, veterans and their families into one place, with a crisis line at the top and no signup anywhere.

Both exist for the same reason: the information is real, it is genuinely hard to find, and nobody was going to be paid to fix that.
