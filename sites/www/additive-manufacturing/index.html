THE RIGHT PROCESS FOR THE JOB

Fit comes first.
So do the requirements.

A part is more than a shape. Tell us about its loads, temperature, exposure, mating surfaces, and how many you need. Those details guide the design and manufacturing route.

Have a model? Start with a STEP or STL file. Have a problem instead? A sketch, photographs, and the critical dimensions are a useful starting point.

Project-specific scope

Materials, process, build size, tolerances, quantity, and lead time are confirmed per project. Metal printing, certified production, and safety-critical qualification are not advertised as available services.

BEFORE WE START

A few useful details.

Do I need a finished CAD file?

No. An existing model helps, but a sketch, measurements, and a description of the problem can start a design discussion.

Is this only for engineering or electronics?

No. We welcome projects across Hawaiʻi: a custom object for home, a shop fixture, a design model, a creative piece, a product prototype, or a small batch for your business. We’ll help determine whether additive manufacturing fits the job.

What materials and quantities are available?

These depend on the requirements and confirmed manufacturing route. Contact us with the intended use and quantity before treating a design as production-ready.
