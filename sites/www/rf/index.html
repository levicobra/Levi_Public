BUILT WITH CONTEXT

Good RF work starts
with the right questions.

What frequencies matter? What else is operating nearby? How will the system be powered, housed, and maintained? What does the operator actually need to know?

We work through these requirements first, then connect the electronics, mechanical design, and software into a coherent prototype.

Current development stage

Our receive-only monitoring work is at prototype stage: a software demonstration and engineering design. Live hardware validation, environmental qualification, and deployment authorization remain ahead. No fielded product or completed customer deployment is claimed.
