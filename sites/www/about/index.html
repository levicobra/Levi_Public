A LOCAL POINT OF VIEW

Small studio.
Practical ambition.

XP Labs is an independent studio founded by Levi Colby in Honolulu, Hawaiʻi. We focus on additive manufacturing for the wide range of things Hawaiʻi needs, with RF engineering and software as additional specialties.

WHY THESE DISCIPLINES

Local needs.
Practical possibilities.

A business needs a custom fixture. A designer needs a physical model. A household needs a part that isn’t on the shelf. An inventor needs a first prototype.

Additive manufacturing gives those needs a place to start. We work through the design, intended use, and manufacturing approach with you. RF engineering and software support projects that need them.

We’re building around Hawaiʻi, with a practical approach to distance, environment, iteration, and local collaboration.
