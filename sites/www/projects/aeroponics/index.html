XP LABS / PROJECT 01

AEROPONICS TOWERS

Room to grow.
Layer by layer.

Our first project brings additive manufacturing into growing. We’re exploring a modular tower designed around compact spaces, replaceable parts, and practical upkeep.

Concept stage

Not yet offered for sale. The illustration shows a design direction, not a tested or finished XP Labs tower.

THE DESIGN BRIEF

Small footprint.
Thoughtful parts.

01 / MODULARITY

Build around the part.

Explore individual tower sections and planting pockets that can be iterated without redesigning the whole system.

02 / EVERYDAY USE

Think beyond the print.

Consider assembly, access for cleaning, stability, and replacement parts from the beginning.

03 / VALIDATION

Let testing lead.

Evaluate the materials, water delivery, sealing, and growing performance before making product claims.

WHAT COMES NEXT

Make. Measure.
Learn. Repeat.

The next steps are defining the design, building prototypes, and documenting what works. Final dimensions, plant capacity, materials, pricing, and availability have not been announced.

Clear expectations

No validated claims are made about water savings, yield, food-contact suitability, outdoor durability, or commercial readiness. Those require evidence from the actual design and materials.
