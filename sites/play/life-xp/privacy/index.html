Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026 · Publisher: XPLabs LLC

XPLabs receives no data about you at all. Your saved game lives on your device and in your own iCloud. The app does show ads, and the advertising network behind them collects data — that section is below, in plain terms, because it is the part that matters.

1. Scope

This policy explains how XPLabs LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in the Life XP iPhone app, these pages, and direct support messages. Apple separately processes App Store downloads, purchases, diagnostics, and account data under Apple’s own policies and agreements.

2. What we collect

Nothing.

XPLabs operates no servers for this app, has no account system, and receives no data about you or your play. We could not identify a single player of this game if we wanted to.

3. Your saved game

Characters, progress, and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, a backup copy is also written to your own iCloud key-value storage, so progress survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone.

That copy belongs to your Apple ID. It syncs between Apple and your devices. It never reaches us, and we have no way to read it.

4. Advertising

Life XP shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To select and measure those ads, Google’s software collects data from your device. We do not receive that data — Google does — but you should know what it is:

  • Device advertising identifier — selecting and measuring ads.
  • Advertising data (which ads were shown, which were tapped) — measuring ads.
  • Coarse location, approximate and derived from your IP address — region-appropriate ads.
  • Product interaction, meaning the fact that an ad was displayed — measuring ads.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — keeping the ad software working.

This qualifies as tracking under Apple’s definition, because the advertising identifier can be linked to your activity in other companies’ apps. What Google does with it is governed by their policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ads appear roughly once per ten minutes of actual gameplay, only at a pause in play, and never during a decision.

5. Your choices

  • Decline tracking. iOS asks your permission before the advertising identifier can be used. Decline and you still get ads, just less relevant ones. Change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove ads entirely. The shop inside the game offers a one-off purchase that removes every ad permanently, on every device signed into your Apple ID. Buying it stops all of the collection described in section 4.
  • In the EEA or UK, a consent form appears before any personalised ad is served, and your choice is respected. You can reopen it from the game’s shop.

6. Purchases

The single in-app purchase, “Remove Ads,” is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details, or address — only whether the purchase exists, which Apple confirms to the app on your device.

7. These pages

These pages contain no scripts, cookies, tracking pixels, external fonts, or remote images of ours, and we receive nothing at all from your visit — no analytics, no logs, no record that you were here.

They are served through Cloudflare, our hosting provider, which may add its own security scripts and a bot-detection cookie at the edge before the page reaches you. That is Cloudflare acting as the host, it is not analytics, and none of it is shared with us or with any advertiser.

8. Children

Life XP is rated 13+ and is not directed at children. Mature systems — crime, substances, dependence and weapons — are switched off by default and can only be enabled with the device owner's Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the effective date above changes and the App Store privacy details are updated to match.
