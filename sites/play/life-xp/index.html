Life XP

Build a life.
Live with the choices.

Behind every door is another way to spend the time you have.

01

Go where life happens

Walk to an office to apply for work, a school to study, or a bank to speak to a teller. Homes, shops, clinics and other destinations have their own interiors and stations.

02

Spend your hours

A shift, a class, a workout, a night's sleep. Studying an afternoon means not earning through it. Your choices connect work, education, housing, health and relationships.

03

Let Monday settle it

Paychecks arrive, living costs come due and interest accumulates. Every week settles in a fixed order, reflecting the decisions you already made.

Consequences,
on your terms.

Mature systems—including crime, substances and their consequences—can be switched off at character creation. Play begins at 18. For help or information about the game, visit support and the privacy policy.
