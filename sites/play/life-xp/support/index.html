Common questions

I reinstalled the app and my character is gone

Progress is backed up to your own iCloud, so it returns on its own the first time you open the app while signed into the same Apple ID. It can arrive a few seconds after the character-creation screen appears — that is normal, and the game will switch back to your life on its own.

If it does not come back, check Settings → [your name] → iCloud and confirm iCloud Drive is on, then relaunch. Still nothing? Email me before starting a new life — starting over overwrites the save slot.

I bought Remove Ads and I am still seeing ads

Open the shop in your profile and tap Restore Purchases. That asks Apple directly and is the fastest fix. If ads continue after that, email me with the Apple receipt and I will sort it out.

How often do ads appear?

About one for every ten minutes you actually spend playing — time the app spends in the background does not count. They only appear at a pause, such as a week settling or leaving a building, and never during a decision. Remove Ads in the shop removes them permanently on every device signed into your Apple ID.

How do I turn off the crime, drugs, and weapons content?

Switch off Mature systems when you create a character. That content is then hidden entirely.

I want to change my tracking or ad-personalisation choice

Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Life XP. Declining still lets you play and still shows ads, just less relevant ones. In the EEA or UK you can also reopen the consent form from the shop.

My character died and the game ended

That is the game working as intended — a life runs out. The summary screen offers to begin another, and your achievements are not lost.

Can I have my data deleted?

There is nothing on our side to delete: XPLabs stores no data about you at all. Your save lives on your device and in your own iCloud. Deleting the app removes the local copy, and you can remove the iCloud copy in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Report a bug, or ask for something
