Frequently asked questions

How do matches damage enemies?

Swap with any neighboring core, including diagonally, then match three or more in a row or column, form an intersecting composite shape, or fill a 2×2 block with one material. Regular clears bank Aurora charge. When the Match Phase ends, Aurora fires one concentrated shot only through the ship’s highlighted lane, regardless of where the matches were made. Tap an upper lane to move the ship before the shot fires.

What is the glowing bar above the board?

A successful match starts the finite Match Phase and stays visibly locked on the board. Keep making new matches or extend a locked group to refill the timer, raise your combo, and charge the upcoming shot. The bar becomes brighter and changes color as time runs short.

Why did the enemies move closer?

The HUD shows a real-time enemy-action clock. When it reaches zero, most enemies move one rank, while Rams alternate a two-rank dash with a recharge. The clock freezes during board and combat presentation, while paused, and during an interruption. An enemy reaching the barrier damages it and leaves the formation.

What do special cores do?

A connected five-core match creates a laser, six creates a drone core, seven creates a plasma bomb, and eight or more creates a singularity. The special appears at the last core added to that group and becomes stronger at each tier. Tap a special core twice, or swap it with any neighboring core, to activate it; specials can chain and are the only attacks that may strike outside Aurora’s selected lane.

Does the game save an unfinished mission?

Yes. Pause confirms that the current board is saved locally, and Space Glyph normally resumes the exact in-progress mission after relaunch. If the app closes on a victory or failure result, that result returns until you claim the victory, retry the loss, or deliberately replace the saved mission. The app also keeps a local backup generation to recover from an interrupted primary save.

Is there cloud save?

No. Space Glyph 1.0 stores campaign progress on this installation and does not sync it between devices. Deleting the app removes that progress, and reinstalling begins a new voyage; support cannot retrieve it from a server. Progress is included in an iPhone backup, so restoring this device or transferring to a new iPhone normally keeps it. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account rather than to the save, so they are unaffected.

How do I restore a permanent purchase?

Open the Store tab and choose Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple Account that originally purchased the item. The campaign remains playable if the Store is unavailable. If the item still does not appear, email support with the product name, approximate purchase time, app version, and any message shown—never your password or card details.

Do purchases make the game easier?

No. Space Glyph’s launch purchases are permanent visual styles. They do not add damage, moves, rewards, better puzzle odds, or campaign access. Stardust is earned by play.

How do refunds work?

Apple processes App Store billing and refund requests. Use Apple’s official Report a Problem service. We can separately investigate whether Space Glyph displayed or granted an item correctly, but cannot promise Apple’s refund decision.

Can I turn off music, haptics, or motion?

Yes. From the campaign map, open Settings with the gear button. Music, effects, volumes, haptics, motion, lane guidance, auto-pause, contrast, material distinction, puzzle-orb size, spoken hints, and color-vision palette have controls. Space Glyph also follows the system Reduce Motion preference.

Does Space Glyph track me?

No. Space Glyph has no advertising tracker or developer analytics server. Optional coarse gameplay counters remain on your device and can be disabled for future recording or deleted separately in Settings. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

The app closed or a mission looks wrong. What should I send?

Email the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, mission number, what you did just before the problem, and what you expected. A screenshot or recording can help if you are comfortable sharing it; hide notifications and personal details first. Do not delete the app as a generic fix because local progress may be lost.

Compatibility

Space Glyph 1.0 is designed and supported for portrait iPhone on iOS 17 or later. It has no native iPad, Mac, or visionOS layout.
