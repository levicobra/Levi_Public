Keep the chain alive

Your first match starts a short Match Phase. Follow-up matches refill its timer, giving you a chance to build a formation before it resolves.

Choose your lane

Aim Aurora to concentrate regular matches into a shot down the selected lane. Larger connected matches forge power gems that can attack across lanes.

Cross four sectors over a 100-mission campaign, with a commander encounter every twenty-five missions. The threat keeps its own real-time clock.
