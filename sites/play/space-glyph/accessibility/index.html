Accessibility in Space Glyph

Space Glyph combines system-aware behavior with player-facing controls for motion, visibility, color distinction, guidance, sound, and haptics.

Find the settings

From the campaign map, choose the gear button in the top-right corner. During a fresh first mission, choose Pause → Leave for Map, then open the gear button. Changes save locally.

Motion and flashing

  • Motion can follow the iOS Reduce Motion setting or use the in-game Reduced option; the in-game Full option never overrides the system accessibility setting.
  • System Reduce Motion stops decorative star twinkle and shortens transitions. Reduced gameplay presentation shortens travel-heavy animation and reduces glow/burst intensity.
  • A persistent lane guide can remain visible to reduce reliance on moving aim feedback.
  • Optional Auto-pause pauses an active mission when the app becomes inactive and waits for the player to resume.

Visibility and color

  • High Contrast strengthens gameplay presentation.
  • Differentiate Without Color adds letter codes to the four puzzle materials.
  • Color-vision palettes include Standard, Deuteranopia, Protanopia, Tritanopia, and Monochrome.
  • Larger Puzzle Orbs increases the physical pieces while retaining clear gutters.
  • Gold, silver, malachite, and obsidian use distinct reflection, banding, tone, and spoken names, so color is not the only cue.

Sound and haptics

  • Music and effects can be disabled independently and have separate volume controls.
  • Haptics can be disabled.
  • Core objectives, moves, countdown, barrier state, and results remain visible without sound or haptics.

Spoken access and controls

SwiftUI navigation and settings controls include accessibility labels. Gameplay exposes a semantic five-row by seven-lane board with lane threat summaries; each orb reports its material, row, lane, selection, and special state. A player can select or deselect a core, swap with an adjacent core, and activate a selected special through the semantic controls. Spoken announcements report selection, accepted or rejected accessibility moves, and mission outcomes. Extra context-sensitive screen-reader hints can be enabled.

Current boundary

Do not interpret this page as a claim that every mission is fully playable with VoiceOver or that every primary text element reaches Apple’s Larger Text criterion. Those App Store accessibility declarations are published only after a player can complete every common task under Apple’s full evaluation criteria. No formal App Store accessibility feature declarations are being made for version 1.0 until physical-device testing is complete on the final release candidate.
