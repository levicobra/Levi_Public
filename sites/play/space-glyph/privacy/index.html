Space Glyph

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026 · Publisher: XPLabs LLC

Space Glyph is designed to keep gameplay data on your device. The app has no advertising tracker, no developer analytics server, and no player account.

1. Scope

This policy explains how XPLabs LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in the Space Glyph iPhone app, this support website, and direct support messages. Apple separately processes App Store downloads, purchases, diagnostics, and account data under Apple’s policies and agreements.

2. Information in the app

The app stores the following information locally in its private app container:

  • Campaign progress, unlocked/completed missions, scores, earned Stardust, Aurora restoration, statistics, owned/selected cosmetic styles, and onboarding state.
  • An in-progress mission, including the puzzle board, enemies, moves, barrier, score, and deterministic state needed to resume it exactly.
  • Your audio, haptic, motion, visibility, color, guidance, purchase-confirmation, and local-analytics preferences.
  • A local purchase ledger and entitlement state used to apply verified StoreKit purchases once and reconcile permanent purchases.

We do not automatically receive this local gameplay information. Version 1.0 has no cloud save or cross-device campaign sync.

3. Local gameplay analytics

If Local Gameplay Analytics is enabled in Settings, Space Glyph records coarse aggregate counters on your device to help with balancing and quality checks. These records use a UTC calendar day, an allow-listed event, broad source/result categories, sector, a five-level band, counts, and bounded numeric totals. They do not include your name, email, advertising identifier, device fingerprint, location, contacts, free-form text, or precise event time.

The app does not automatically transmit these counters. It prunes records under a 90-day retention rule. Turning the setting off stops future recording; it does not itself erase existing records. You can choose Delete Local Analytics in Settings to remove the primary and backup aggregate files without changing campaign progress or purchases. Deleting the app also removes its local analytics under normal iOS behavior.

4. Tracking and advertising

Space Glyph contains no advertising SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. It does not request permission through App Tracking Transparency. We do not sell or share app gameplay information for targeted advertising.

5. App Store purchases

Space Glyph offers optional permanent cosmetic in-app purchases. Apple processes the transaction. Space Glyph uses StoreKit to request product information, receive Apple-signed transaction and entitlement status, grant the purchased permanent cosmetic locally, restore eligible purchases, and respond to revocation. We do not receive your full payment-card or bank details, and the app does not send transaction data to a publisher server.

Apple retains and handles App Store information under its own privacy policy. You can learn more at Apple Privacy.

6. Website and support messages

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, contact form, external fonts, remote images, JavaScript, or non-essential cookies. Hosting infrastructure may process basic request information such as IP address, date/time, requested page, and browser information for delivery, security, and abuse prevention. These pages are hosted on Cloudflare Pages and delivered through the Cloudflare network; Cloudflare may process routine request and security logs for those purposes. XPLabs does not add analytics or advertising scripts to these pages.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as app version, device/OS, mission number, description, and attachments. Support email is handled through Google Gmail. We use the information to answer, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and improve the app. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details, or information unrelated to your request. Support records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain security, resolve a dispute, keep required accounting records, or meet legal obligations. They are then deleted or de-identified under our retention process.

7. Disclosure and legal needs

We do not sell support information. We may use service providers needed to operate our support email and static hosting, subject to the purposes above. Those providers currently include Google for Gmail and Cloudflare for this website’s hosting and delivery. We may preserve or disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud/security, or establish or defend legal claims.

8. Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • Campaign, settings, and purchase-ledger data remain locally until changed or the app’s data is deleted.
  • Local analytics follow the 90-day rule above, can be disabled for future recording, and can be deleted separately in Settings.
  • Deleting Space Glyph removes local app data under normal iOS behavior. Because 1.0 has no cloud save, campaign progress may not be recoverable.
  • Apple may retain download, purchase, and entitlement records under its own policies. Permanent eligible purchases can be reconciled using Restore Purchases.
  • You may contact us about a support record at xp.labs.3d@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request while collecting as little additional information as practical.

There is no Space Glyph player account, so there is no developer-held game account to delete.

9. Children

Space Glyph is a general-audience game and is not offered in Apple’s Kids Category. The app does not knowingly collect personal information through a developer account, advertising, chat, or analytics server. If you believe a child sent personal information in a support message, contact us so we can address it appropriately.

10. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information we handle. No method is completely secure. Local app information is protected by the iOS app container and device controls. Support and hosting information may be processed in the United States and other locations where Google and Cloudflare operate. Contact us if you have questions about processing related to your support request.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, law, or our practices change. We will post the new effective date here and provide additional notice when required. Features such as cloud save, accounts, online analytics, advertising, social tools, or remote live operations will require an updated policy and App Store disclosures before launch.
