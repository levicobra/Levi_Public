Hearth & Hunt

A little cozy.
A little competitive.

Stay close to the hearth or see what is waiting beyond it. Roles switch freely.

01

Keep the hearth

As a Keeper, grow magical crops and improve the homestead. Make a place worth coming back to while your team's next expedition takes shape.

02

Hunt the wilds

As a Hunter, fight creatures, contest objectives and bring rare materials home. Meadows, highlands and summits offer rising rewards for rising risk.

03

Meet at the Bloom

A fifteen-minute match ends in the Grand Bloom. Play solo or as a duo, with deliberately non-destructive, low-punishment player-versus-player encounters.
