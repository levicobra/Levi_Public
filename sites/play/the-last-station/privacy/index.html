The Last Station

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026 · Publisher: XPLabs LLC

The Last Station keeps your progress on your device. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

The short version. The game runs offline. Your save file lives on your iPhone and is never uploaded to us. There are no ads, no trackers, and no third-party analytics. The only information that reaches anyone is the purchase record Apple creates if you buy something — and Apple, not us, handles that.

1. Scope

This policy explains how XPLabs LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in the The Last Station iPhone app, these pages, and direct support messages. Apple separately processes App Store downloads, purchases, and diagnostics under its own policies.

2. What we collect

From the app: nothing that identifies you. The Last Station does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. It does not create an account for you, because there are no accounts.

  • Game progress — level, currency, unlocked cards and skins, run history. Stored on your device. We never receive it.
  • Settings — audio, control, and accessibility choices. Stored on your device. We never receive it.
  • In-app purchase records — held by Apple against your Apple Account. StoreKit verifies Apple's signed transaction on your device, and the game keeps a local transaction record so it cannot grant the same purchase twice. We do not receive it.
  • Crash and performance data — collected by Apple only if you opted in under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics, and shared with us aggregated and anonymised, through Apple.

3. In-app purchases

When you buy Cores or a bundle, Apple processes the payment. We never see your card number, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. StoreKit cryptographically verifies Apple's signed transaction on your device before the game delivers it. The signed transaction and its identifier stay in the game's local save as a delivery and restore record; the app does not send them to an XPLabs server.

4. Camera and microphone

The Last Station does not use your camera or microphone. You may notice the App Store listing mentions them anyway. iOS requires an app to declare a reason for every Apple framework it links, and the game engine we build on links Apple's capture frameworks as part of its standard media stack. The game never opens a capture session and never records anything. If iOS ever prompts you for camera or microphone access while playing, that is a bug — please report it.

5. Children

The Last Station is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to delete — but if you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at xp.labs.3d@gmail.com and we will investigate.

6. Third parties

We do not use advertising networks, attribution SDKs, social login providers, or third-party analytics. We do not sell or share personal information, and never have. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act we do not “sell” or “share” your data as those terms are defined; under the GDPR, we do not process personal data through the app at all.

These pages carry no scripts, cookies, pixels, external fonts, or remote images.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Delete everything. Because your save lives on your device, deleting The Last Station deletes your game data with it. This is permanent — we hold no backup and cannot restore it.
  • Purchase history. Your purchase records belong to your Apple Account. Manage them at Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases.
  • Access, correction, portability. If your jurisdiction grants these rights, write to xp.labs.3d@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. In most cases the honest answer will be that we hold no personal data about you.

If this changes, we will say so. We are building optional cloud saves so progress can follow you to a new device. That feature is not in this release. If and when it ships it will be opt-in, it will require you to choose to sign in, and this policy will be updated before it is switched on.

8. Retention and security

Apple retains App Store purchase records under its own policies. The game retains its signed transaction records only in the local save on your device, where they prevent duplicate delivery and support entitlement restoration. The app does not transmit those records to us. We hold no names, addresses, payment credentials, or game-save backup.

9. Contact

XPLabs LLC, Honolulu, Hawaii, United States — xp.labs.3d@gmail.com. We read every message and aim to reply within two business days.
