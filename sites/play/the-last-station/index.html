The Last Station

A free-to-play survivors game built for one thumb. You only steer — aim is automatic and your equipped cards fire on their own. All the depth lives in the loadout.

Coming to the App Store Support

What it is

You play C, the last operator of a dying signal station on a refurbished offshore rig. Everyone else is gone. The signal is still transmitting.

Expeditions run about three minutes and campaign levels about ninety seconds, so a run fits in the gap you actually have. The pre-mission read is the game: five part cards across Power, Weapons, Sensors, Jammers and Drones, plus five stat medallions. Kinetic weapons break jam-immune brutes, energy weapons melt shields.

Plays offline. No account, no sign-in, no ads. Your save lives on your device.

Odds are published. Every supply pack rarity is listed in-app, and a guaranteed pull lands within 60.

One thumb. Drag anywhere to steer. Nothing else is required of your hands.
