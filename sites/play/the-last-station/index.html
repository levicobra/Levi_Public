Short expeditions. Meaningful preparation. A station at the center of it all.

Build your loadout

Equip five part cards and stat medallions before heading out. Power, weapons, sensors, jammers and drones give you different ways to face the next expedition.

02

Read the threat

Kinetic weapons break jam-immune brutes. Energy weapons melt shields. Choose what you bring, then steer while automatic aim handles the targeting.

03

Make every run count

Expeditions run about three minutes, with shorter campaign levels. A small window of time is enough to step back into the station's world.
