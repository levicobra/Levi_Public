Support

Publisher: XPLabs LLC · Honolulu, Hawaii, United States

Something broken, confusing, or missing? Write to us and a person will answer.

Email xp.labs.3d@gmail.com — we aim to reply within two business days. For the fastest useful answer, include your device model, your iOS version, the game version from Settings → About inside the game, and what you were doing when it went wrong. A screenshot or screen recording helps more than anything else.

I bought something and did not receive it

First, force-quit the game and reopen it — an interrupted purchase usually completes on the next launch. If it still has not arrived, open Store → Restore Purchases. If neither works, email us with the receipt Apple sent you and we will make it right.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we never receive your payment. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account that made the purchase. We cannot approve, deny, or speed up Apple's decision, but if a purchase failed because of a bug on our side, tell us and we will say so to Apple.

I lost my progress

The Last Station saves your progress on your device. There are no accounts and no cloud saves in this release, which means:

  • Deleting the app deletes your save. It cannot be recovered — not by you and not by us.
  • A new phone does not carry your save across unless you restore it from an encrypted iPhone backup, which does include game data.
  • Purchases can be restored through Apple even when progress cannot. Use Store → Restore Purchases.

Optional cloud saves are in development. When they ship, they will be opt-in.

Reporting a bug

Please tell us. Small, specific reports are the ones that get fixed. The most useful report names one thing that went wrong, says what you expected instead, and says whether it happens every time or only sometimes.

Accessibility

The Last Station is built to be played one-handed with a single thumb, and supports Dynamic Type, reduced motion, and VoiceOver on menu screens. If something in the game is unusable for you, that is a bug worth reporting — write to xp.labs.3d@gmail.com and describe what you need. We take these first.

Privacy and data

See the privacy policy. The short version is that the game keeps your data on your device and sends us nothing. To delete everything the game holds, delete the app.
