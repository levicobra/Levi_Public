The Last Station

Unreal Engine 5 · mobile · free to play

A survivors game built for one thumb. You only steer — your equipped cards fire on tap and aim is automatic. All the depth is in the loadout: five part cards across Power, Weapons, Sensors, Jammers and Drones, plus five stat medallions. Kinetic weapons break armoured brutes, energy weapons melt shields. That is the whole pre-mission read.

You play C, the last operator of a dying signal station on a refurbished offshore rig. Runs last about three minutes. A restart takes two seconds.

  • 26 cards
  • 8 medallions
  • ~3 min runs

Space Glyph

Swift 6 · SpriteKit · iPhone

Matching and shooting are the same act. Your first match locks the board and starts a short timed phase; every follow-up match refills the timer, so a good chain is something you extend under pressure. Bigger connected matches forge stronger gems — the only attack that crosses lanes.

No ads, no subscriptions, no loot boxes, no tracking. Purchases are cosmetic and never gate progress.

  • 100 missions
  • 4 sectors

Life XP

SwiftUI · SceneKit · iPhone

An offline life simulator. Walk a small town, step through any door into a full-screen interior, and use the station that belongs there — a bed, a teller window, a weight rack, a gig board.

Underneath, a deterministic hour-by-hour simulation ties together work, school, banking, housing, health and consequences. Every Monday the world settles up in a fixed order. Money is tracked in whole cents so nothing drifts.

  • 17 locations
  • 12 jobs
  • Fully offline

Hearth & Hunt

Roblox · server-authoritative PvPvE

Built for two-player teams who want something relaxed and solo players who want something competitive — in the same match. Keepers stay home, grow magical crops and upgrade the homestead. Hunters go out into the wilds, fight creatures, contest objectives and haul rare materials back. Roles switch freely.

Three terrain bands pay 1×, 2× and 3×, so the risk you take is the reward you get. PvP is deliberately non-destructive and low-punishment. Everything resolves in a fifteen-minute match ending in the Grand Bloom.

  • 15 min matches
  • Solo or duo
  • Keeper · Hunter

XP Education

Offline learning platform · installs as an app

A whole school that works with no connection. Visit once and the entire library — 106 subjects across 14 domains, from K–8 reading and arithmetic through college calculus, chemistry, accounting and Japanese — lives on your device. Real lessons, worked examples, key terms and practice questions, not link lists.

College courses follow the scope and sequence of OpenStax open textbooks and link to the full free book. Progress is stored on your device. There are no accounts, no tracking and no paywall, and there is nothing to buy.
